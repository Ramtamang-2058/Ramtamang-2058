### 🔍 About Me

I build systems that warn before the flood arrives. Currently a **Backend Engineer at [NAXA](https://naxa.com.np)**,
working on **Nepal's national weather forecast and flood early-warning platforms** serving governments and
humanitarian teams across three countries.

- 🔭 Building the backend of Nepal's national weather forecast system for the Department of Hydrology & Meteorology — ECMWF, NOAA GFS, ICON-EPS and GLOFAS feeds turned into SMS/IVR alerts before rivers rise
- 🗺️ Owner of the **National Geoportal of Nepal** — led the GeoNode 3.x → 4.4.5 migration and production cutover
- 🚨 Real-time flooding alerts for **[DASTAA](https://dastaa.org/)** — operational in Nepal, Bangladesh, and Malawi
- 🤖 **Software lead** on Nepal's first open-source humanoid robot (InMoov) at HTP Lab — vision, speech, LLM reasoning
- 🥅 Constantly setting new goals and challenges for myself

Previously: VoIP microservices at **KrispCall** (YC-backed, 99.9% uptime for 10,000+ concurrent users),
Django apps at the **Robotics Association of Nepal**.
